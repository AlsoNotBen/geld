Income Statement

The income statement is the statement of profit or loss. It shows the revenue of one year, then the costs that the business paid to earn it, and the profit that remains. The statement reads from top to bottom: revenue, then cost of sales, then the operating expenses, then finance and tax. Each subtotal answers one question, for example: what is the margin on the goods, and what does the business keep after tax. The second table adds the gains that do not go through profit, for example a revaluation. The year is to 31 March 2026, with the year to 31 March 2025 for comparison. All figures in ZAR.
