Leads

A lead is an entity that is not yet a customer. The board shows the open stages of the sales cycle: New, Prospected, Qualified, Engaged and Awaiting. The table below the board shows the closed leads. A card moves to the right as the lead moves through the cycle, and the gauges of the card fill. A green dot means that the next step is yours. A yellow dot means that you wait for the lead. Click a card to see the full record.

Click a card to open the lead.

{% comment %} ------------------------------------------------------------------ THE BOARD The view gives one column for each open stage of Lead.SaleStage. The Closed stage is a table below the board. Each card holds its data in the data-* attributes of _lead_attrs.html. leads.js copies them into the overlay. A gauge appears only if the lead has a value for it: Size 1 to 1000 Qualifying 0 to 100 Temperature one step for each choice, Ice to Hot Response full at the last response, empty after 7 days A column shows five cards. A card after the fifth carries the hidden attribute, and the button at the foot of the column shows the rest. The count pill sits next to that button, thus a short column shows no count. ------------------------------------------------------------------ {% endcomment %}
