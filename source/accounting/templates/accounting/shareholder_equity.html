Statement of Changes in Equity

This statement shows how the equity of the owners changed in one year. Equity is the part of the business that belongs to the owners after all debts. Each column is one equity account. Each row is one movement: a profit adds to equity, a dividend takes from it, and a share issue brings new capital in. The last column is the total of each row, and the last row is the total equity at the end of the year. That figure must agree with the balance sheet. An owner reads this statement to see what the business kept and what it paid out. The year is to 31 March 2026, with the year to 31 March 2025 for comparison. All figures in ZAR.

Notes to the statement

  1. 1. Basis of preparation

    The company prepares the statement in agreement with IFRS for SMEs. The figures show the movement of each equity account for the year to 31 March 2026.

    The company applied the same accounting policies in the two years. Thus the reader can compare the two years.

  2. 2. Share capital and share premium

    The company issued 250,000 ordinary shares on 20 February 2026. The issue price was 2.22 per share.

    The company put 300,000 to share capital and 255,000 to share premium. The number of shares in issue increased from 1,000,000 to 1,250,000.

  3. 3. Share issue costs

    The costs of the share issue were 15,000. These costs are the fees of the transfer secretary and the legal fees. The company subtracted the costs from share premium.

  4. 4. Revaluation reserve

    An independent valuer examined the land and the buildings on 28 February 2026. The gain before tax was 164,384.

    The deferred tax on the gain was 44,384. Thus the reserve increased by 120,000. The company will not move this gain to profit or loss.

  5. 5. Dividends

    The board declared a dividend of 28.8 cents per share on 28 February 2026. The dividend was 360,000 on 1,250,000 shares.

    The dividend of the last year was 24.0 cents per share, or 240,000 on 1,000,000 shares. The company paid all dividends before the end of the year.

  6. 6. Retained earnings

    Retained earnings include the profit for the year of 1,262,000. The balance of 3,592,000 is available for distribution.

    The company holds no restriction on this balance. The loan agreement in note 12 of the annual statements sets a minimum equity of 5,000,000.
