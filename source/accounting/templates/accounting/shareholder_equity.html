Statement of Changes in Equity

This statement shows how the equity of the owners changed in one year. Equity is the part of the business that belongs to the owners after all debts. Each column is one equity account. Each row is one movement: a profit adds to equity, a dividend takes from it, and a share issue brings new capital in. The last column is the total of each row, and the last row is the total equity at the end of the year. That figure must agree with the balance sheet. An owner reads this statement to see what the business kept and what it paid out.
