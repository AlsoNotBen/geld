Overview

The overview is the first page of the Accounting module. It shows the position of the business today: the money that customers owe, the money that is late, the money that the business owes to suppliers, and the cash in the bank. The Balance, Income and Cash Flow parts show the results of the three statements and compare this period with the last period. The table at the bottom shows the last entries in the books. Use this page to see if an item needs an action before you open a report.
