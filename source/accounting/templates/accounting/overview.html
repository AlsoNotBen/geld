Overview

The overview shows the position of the business and the work that waits. The three charts show the cash, the invoices and the bills at the end of the date range. The table shows the items that need an action, and the stream shows who did what. Each card shows an amount at the end of the range, with a graph of the range. The invoices and the bills that were paid in full show for the range only. The last part shows the company on a map, the public notes of all pages, and the movement against the previous period. The date range at the top right is for this page only. Click a chart, a row, a card or a note to open its page.
