Journal Entries

A journal is the first record of a transaction. Each entry names the date, the reference, the account and the amount. The four journals below sort the entries by type: general, sales, purchases and cash. The list at the top holds the entries that a person must review. Accept an entry to post it to the general ledger, or edit it first. Only accepted entries go into the statements.
