Journal Entries

A journal is a detailed running record of all of a business's financial transactions. It is used to reconcile accounts and is transferred to other accounting records, such as the general ledger. The journal includes information like the transaction date, the accounts affected, and the cash amounts. Journal entries are composed in a double-entry bookkeeping method.
