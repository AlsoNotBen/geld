Chart of Accounts

The chart of accounts is the list of all accounts in the books. Each account has a code, a name and a type. The five tabs sort the accounts into the five categories of the balance sheet and the income statement: assets, liabilities, equity, income and expenses. Every journal entry must go to one of these accounts. Use this page to add an account, to change its name, or to archive an account that the business no longer uses. All balances in ZAR.
