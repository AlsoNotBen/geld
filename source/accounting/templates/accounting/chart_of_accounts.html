Chart of Accounts

The chart of accounts is the list of all accounts in the books. Each account has a code, a name and a type. The tabs sort the accounts into the five categories of the balance sheet and the income statement: assets, liabilities, equity, income and expenses. A floating nominal account, e.g. VAT control, goes to assets when it has a debit balance, and to liabilities when it has a credit balance. Every journal entry must go to one of these accounts. All balances in ZAR.

Select a row to edit or archive an account.
