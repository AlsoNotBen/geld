{% extends "accounting/module_base.html" %} {% load static fin %} {% comment %} ============================================================================ accounting/templates/accounting/claims.html ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- An employee submits a claim: a Document with the Claim type (doc), with its Claim (doc.claim) and one line for each receipt (reason, account, amount, files). The page has two parts: .review the draft claims. Approve posts the entry of the claim. View shows the claim, and its Deny button voids it. Accept All approves all of them. .tabset the approved claims: Open (the default) and Paid. The Pay (or Receive, for an insurance claim) icon opens the Record Payment overlay with the claim number as the reference (payment_form.js). The payment can pay all or a part of the open amount. It makes draft entries. The claim is Paid (or Partially paid) when the entries are accepted in Journal Entries (see posting.allocate and update_claims). Only an Owner or an Accountant sees the Approve, Deny, Pay and Receive buttons. The New Claim button opens #claim-dialog. Its three tabs set the kind of the claim (Expense is the default), and the default claim account of the kind (see posting.CLAIM_ACCOUNTS). claims.js adds and removes the line rows and sends the form with fetch(). ============================================================================ {% endcomment %} {% block page_key %}claims{% endblock %} {% block title %}Claims · Accounting · Console{% endblock %} {% block toolbar %}{{ block.super }}{% endblock %} {% block content %}
