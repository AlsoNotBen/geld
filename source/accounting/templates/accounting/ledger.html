General Ledger

The general ledger is the full record of the books. It holds one account for each line of the chart of accounts, and it lists every accepted journal entry under the account that it touched. Each table starts with the balance brought forward from the last period, shows each debit and credit of this period, and keeps a running balance. The closing balance of each account goes into the balance sheet or the income statement. An auditor reads the ledger to trace a figure in a statement back to its entry.
