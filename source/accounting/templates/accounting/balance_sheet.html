Balance Sheet

The balance sheet is the statement of financial position. It shows what the business owns (the assets), what it owes (the liabilities) and what remains for the owners (the equity) on one day. The two totals must agree: total assets are equal to total equity and liabilities. A lender reads this statement to see if the business can pay its debts. An owner reads it to see the value of the business. The position is at {{ end|date:"j F Y" }}, with {{ prev_end|date:"j F Y" }} for comparison.
