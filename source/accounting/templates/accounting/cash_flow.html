Cash Flow Statement{% notes_pill %}

The cash flow statement shows where the cash came from and where it went in one year. A profit is not the same as cash: a customer can owe money, and a machine can lose value without a payment. Thus the statement starts at the profit and corrects it, until it gets to the change in the bank. It sorts the movements into three groups: the daily work of the business, the purchase and sale of long-term items, and the money between the business, its lenders and its owners. A manager reads this statement to see if the business makes enough cash to pay its bills.
