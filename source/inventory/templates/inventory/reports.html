Planned reports

  • Stock on Hand

    The quantity and the value of every item, by location, at a date that you choose. The report is the base of the stock figure in the balance sheet.

  • Stock Movement

    Every receipt, issue, transfer and adjustment of a period, with its reference and its person. The report answers the question: where did the stock go?

  • Asset Register

    Every asset with its tag, its holder, its place and its state. An auditor asks for this report first.

  • Reorder List

    The items that stand below the minimum level, with the quantity that a new order needs. The list becomes an order in the Accounting module.

  • Consumable Usage

    The quantity that each cost centre consumed in a period. The report shows which work uses the most material.

  • Stock Take Difference

    The difference between the count on the shelf and the count in the books, with the value of each difference.
