Overview

The overview is the first page of the Inventory module. It shows what the business holds today: the value of the stock, the number of assets, the number of items of each type, and the items that are empty. The three lists below show the items that need an action, the items that came in last, and the last movements of stock. Use this page to find an item that needs an action before you open a list.

The figures show all items on {{ today|date:"d F Y" }}.
