Kit & Components

A component is a part that the work puts into a larger item. A kit is the item that the parts make. The bill of materials tells you which components one kit needs, and how many of each. When you assemble a kit, the stock of each component goes down and the stock of the kit goes up. The list below shows the kits first, then the components that the kits use.
