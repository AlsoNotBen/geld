The page has three columns that work together: left the kits. The pill gives the number of kits that the stock on hand can build now. The recipe of the kit and the stock of each component give this number. The component with the smallest number sets it. middle the assembly map of the kit that the user selected. It is a graph: a node is a component, and an edge goes from a parent to a child with the quantity on it. dagre gives the layout, and bom-graph.js puts the nodes on the canvas. The form at the foot adds a node. right the components of the same kit, with the quantity in stock. The columns are .card elements. The two lists are .nav__item elements of the side panel. The pills are .status elements. Only the map and the grid of the three columns are new. Refer to inventory.css. The map needs two files: inventory/static/inventory/vendor/dagre.min.js inventory/static/inventory/js/bom-graph.js Both come from the static directory. The page loads no CDN. The view gives the kits (the items that contain other items), the selected kit (?kit=pk, else the first kit), its graph and its direct components. The number that a kit can build is the smallest quotient of the quantity on hand of a direct component and the quantity that one kit needs (see buildable in views.py). ============================================================================ {% endcomment %} {% block page_key %}bom{% endblock %} {% block title %}Bill of Materials · Inventory · Console{% endblock %} {% block content %}

Bill of Materials

A bill of materials tells you which components one kit needs, and how many of each. A component can hold components of its own, thus the tree has more than one level. Select a kit at the left to see its tree. The pill of a kit gives the number that the stock can build now. The component with the smallest number sets that figure, thus the pill falls when one shelf is empty.
