Bill of Materials

A bill of materials tells you which components one kit needs, and how many of each. A component can hold components of its own, thus the tree has more than one level. Select a kit at the left to see its tree. The pill of a kit gives the number that the stock can build now. The component with the smallest number sets that figure, thus the pill falls when one shelf is empty.

Radio Field Kit can build 4. The charging dock sets the limit.
