Items

An item is a row of one kind: an asset, a stock item, a consumable or a component. The type of an item does not change what an item is made of; a relationship does that. A relationship joins two items, a parent and a child, with a type and a quantity. A bill of materials, an accessory, a replacement and a compatible part are all the same kind of row, with a different type. Select an item at the left to edit it, or to change what it relates to.
