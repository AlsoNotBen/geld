The database holds one table for every kind of item: an asset, a stock item, a consumable or a component. The page holds one form for the same reason, and a select sets the type. A relationship joins two items: a parent and a child, of one of five types, with a quantity. The page shows the relationships of the selected item at the right, and a small form adds one more. The three columns reuse .bom, the grid that the Bill of Materials page built first: left the items. Search, filter by type, and select one. middle the form of the selected item: code, name, type, description and the active flag. right the relationships of the selected item, and the form that adds one. A note names the one rule that the middleware checks: a relationship must not close a circle. The view gives the items of the list (?q, ?item_type), the selected item (?item=pk) and its relationships. Without a selection, the form makes a new item. Items

An item is a row of one kind: an asset, a stock item, a consumable or a component. The type of an item does not change what an item is made of; a relationship does that. A relationship joins two items, a parent and a child, with a type and a quantity. A bill of materials, an accessory, a replacement and a compatible part are all the same kind of row, with a different type. Select an item at the left to edit it, or to change what it relates to.
